Dot Widgets Pro turns your home screen into a clean, minimal dot-matrix display. Pick a widget, style it your way, and place it. No widget maker, no extra apps, no setup scripts.

223 WIDGETS, READY TO PLACE
• Clocks: digital dot, analog ring, binary, flip, word clock, world clocks, stacked time and more
• Calendar: day and date, agenda, month grid, week and year progress, life in dots, countdowns
• Weather: current conditions, forecast strips, sunrise and sunset, moon phase, air quality, UV
• Battery and system: battery rings and gauges, charging, connected-device battery, storage, RAM
• Health and focus: steps, habits, mood log, hydration, focus timer, pomodoro, tally counter
• Toggles: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, torch, Do Not Disturb, rotation and quick-action grids
• Plus finance, travel, learning, sports, media controls, photo frames, app shortcuts and smart-home widgets

MAKE EVERY WIDGET YOURS
• 8 skins: Dot, Light Strip, Segment, Pixel, Terminal, Paper, Neon and Native Dot
• 4 frames, adjustable corners, background opacity and text size
• Accent colours, a custom colour picker, or colours from your wallpaper (Material You)
• Dark, light or system theme for each widget
• Time and date formats, time zones and per-widget advanced options
• A live preview shows every change before you place the widget

RESTYLE EVERYTHING AT ONCE
• 10 one-tap Looks repaint every widget on your home screen together
• Curated setups combine widgets, an accent and a matching wallpaper
• 112 dot-matrix wallpapers, palette-matched to your widgets
• Export your setup as a code and share it, or import a friend's

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF THE WAY
• Live seconds and clocks that stay on the minute
• Only widgets that changed are redrawn, which keeps updates light
• Most widgets work offline; online data is cached for when you lose signal
• Scrollable list widgets and a swipeable Smart Stack

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting
• Every permission is optional and asked for only when a widget needs it
• Calendar, contacts, steps and usage data are read on your phone to draw your widgets and are not sent anywhere
• Weather uses your approximate location, or a city you choose

OPTIONAL CONNECTIONS
• Connect your own Home Assistant server for smart-home widgets; your token stays encrypted on your phone
• Add your own sports feed for team score and fixture widgets

HOW TO ADD A WIDGET
Open the app, choose a widget, customise it and tap Add to home screen. Or long-press your home screen, open Widgets and pick Dot Widgets.

Works on Android 7.0 and later. Lock-screen widgets appear only on phones whose lock screen supports widgets from other apps.
